Black Queen: how a game runs

Mockup only. You plus bots, in your browser. Nothing is built yet.

Start to finish

1SetupPick number of players (5 to 9). You take seat 1, the rest are bots.Bots get names; you can switch a seat to "human" later for pass-and-play.
2DealCards per player = floor(52 / players). Extra cards removed, lowest first.
3BidRandom first bidder, clockwise. Raise by 5 or pass. Passing is final. 125 ends it. All pass: last player takes the minimum.
4CallWinner picks trump and names partner card(s). Partners stay hidden until the card is played.
5PlayBidder leads. Follow suit. Highest trump wins, else highest of the led suit. Winner leads next.
6ScoreTeam points ≥ bid: bidder and partners get +bid, opponents 0. Otherwise bidder gets −bid.
↻ After scoring, the scoreboard keeps running totals and a "Deal next round" button starts step 2 again. There is no end; you stop when you want.

F. Bots (my guess, not agreed)

Level 1: simple

Bids from the points in its hand, picks its longest suit as trump, follows suit, plays the lowest card that wins, otherwise dumps its lowest.

Level 2: smarter (later)

Tracks which point cards have gone, guesses who the partner is, and saves points for its own team.
